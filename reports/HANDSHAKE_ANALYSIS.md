## Submit/completion correlation

The native analyzer reports six USBPcap records. Treating them as six live USB
transactions would be incorrect. All six have:

- capture-relative time `0.000000`
- IRP ID `0`
- status `0`
- descriptor/configuration payloads generated by `--inject-descriptors`

They form three ordered synthetic pairs:

| Pair | Record | Direction | Function | Payload | Interpretation |
|---:|---:|---|---:|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | host to device | `0x000B` | `80 06 00 01 00 00 12 00` | GET_DESCRIPTOR(Device) setup |
| 1 | 2 | device to host | `0x0008` | `12 01 00 02 00 00 00 40 83 04 65 52 00 02 01 02 03 01` | injected device descriptor |
| 2 | 3 | host to device | `0x000B` | `80 06 00 02 00 00 2E 00` | GET_DESCRIPTOR(Configuration) setup |
| 2 | 4 | device to host | `0x0008` | `09 02 2E 00 01 01 00 C0 32 09 04 00 00 04 FF FF 00 00 07 05 81 03 10 00 01 07 05 01 03 10 00 01 07 05 82 02 40 00 20 07 05 02 02 40 00 20` | injected configuration descriptor |
| 3 | 5 | host to device | `0x0000` | `00 09 01 00 00 00 00 00` | SET_CONFIGURATION setup |
| 3 | 6 | device to host | `0x0000` | empty | injected completion |

The function values above are USBPcap metadata, not VOD700 command bytes. In
particular, the `0x000B` metadata value must not be confused with vendor command
byte `0x0B`.
